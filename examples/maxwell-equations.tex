\section*{Maxwell equations}

Let $\mathbf E$ be the electric field
\begin{equation*}
\mathbf E=
\begin{pmatrix}
A_0\sin(kz-\omega t)\\0\\0
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

and $\mathbf B$ the magnetic field
\begin{equation*}
\mathbf B=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\A_0\sin(kz-\omega t)\\0
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

where $k=\omega/c$.
Verify $\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B$ are solutions to the free-field Maxwell equations
\begin{align*}
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E&=0
\\
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B&=0
\\[1ex]
\nabla\times\mathbf E&=-\frac{1}{c}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}{\mathbf B}
\\[1ex]
\nabla\times\mathbf B&=\frac{1}{c}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}{\mathbf E}
\end{align*}
