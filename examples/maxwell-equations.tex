\section*{Maxwell equations}

Let
\begin{equation*}
\mathbf E=
\begin{pmatrix}
A_0\sin(kz-\omega t)\\0\\0
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

and
\begin{equation*}
\mathbf B=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\A_0\sin(kz-\omega t)\\0
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

where $k=\omega/c$.
Verify $\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B$ are solutions to the free-field Maxwell equations
\begin{align*}
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E&=0
\\
\nabla\cdot\mathbf B&=0
\\[1ex]
\nabla\times\mathbf E+\frac{1}{c}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}{\mathbf B}&=0
\\[1ex]
\nabla\times\mathbf B-\frac{1}{c}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}{\mathbf E}&=0
\end{align*}
