\section*{Bhabha scattering 4}

From the first section we have
\begin{align*}
\sum_{abcd}\mathcal M_1\mathcal M_1^*&=\frac{e^4}{t^2}f_{11}
\\
\sum_{abcd}\mathcal M_1\mathcal M_2^*&=\frac{e^4}{st}f_{12}
\\
\sum_{abcd}\mathcal M_2\mathcal M_2^*&=\frac{e^4}{s^2}f_{22}
\end{align*}

where
\begin{align*}
f_{11}&=8 s^2 + 8 u^2 - 64 m^2 s - 64 m^2 u + 192 m^4
\\
f_{12}&=-8 u^2 + 64 m^2 u - 96 m^4
\\
f_{22}&=8 t^2 + 8 u^2 - 64 m^2 t - 64 m^2 u + 192 m^4
\end{align*}

Densities can also be formed as
\begin{align*}
f_{11}&=32 (p_1\cdot p_2)^2 + 32 (p_1\cdot p_4)^2 - 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_2) + 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_4)
\\
f_{12}&=-32 (p_1\cdot p_4)^2 - 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_4)
\\
f_{22}&=32 (p_1\cdot p_3)^2 + 32 (p_1\cdot p_4)^2 + 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_3) + 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_4)
\end{align*}

For $m=0$
\begin{align*}
32(p_1\cdot p_2)^2&=8 s^2
\\
32(p_1\cdot p_3)^2&=8 t^2
\\
32(p_1\cdot p_4)^2&=8 u^2
\end{align*}
