\section*{Bloch sphere}

Let $|s\rangle$ be the spin state
\begin{equation*}
|s\rangle=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos(\theta/2)
\\
\sin(\theta/2)\exp(i\phi)
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

The expectation values for $|s\rangle$ are
\begin{align*}
\langle S_x\rangle&=\langle s|S_x|s\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}\sin\theta\cos\phi
\\[1ex]
\langle S_y\rangle&=\langle s|S_y|s\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}\sin\theta\sin\phi
\\[1ex]
\langle S_z\rangle&=\langle s|S_z|s\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}\cos\theta
\end{align*}

In vector form
\begin{equation*}
\langle\mathbf S\rangle=\langle s|\mathbf S|s\rangle
=\frac{\hbar}{2}\begin{pmatrix}
\sin\theta\cos\phi
\\
\sin\theta\sin\phi
\\
\cos\theta
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

In spherical coordinates $\langle\mathbf S\rangle=(\hbar/2,\theta,\phi)$.
Hence $\langle\mathbf S\rangle$ is a point on a sphere of radius $\hbar/2$.

To show completeness, let $|\chi\rangle$ be a vector in $\mathbb C^2$
such that $\langle\chi|\chi\rangle=1$.
\begin{equation*}
|\chi\rangle
=\begin{pmatrix}
A\exp(i\phi_1)
\\
\sqrt{1-A^2}\exp(i\phi_2)
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

Substitute $\cos(\theta/2)$ for $A$ and $\sin(\theta/2)$ for $\sqrt{1-A^2}$.
\begin{equation*}
|\chi\rangle=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos(\theta/2)\exp(i\phi_1)
\\
\sin(\theta/2)\exp(i\phi_2)
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

Spin $|s\rangle$ is a phase of $|\chi\rangle$ with $\phi=\phi_2-\phi_1$.
\begin{equation*}
|s\rangle=\exp(-i\phi_1)|\chi\rangle
=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos(\theta/2)
\\
\sin(\theta/2)\exp(i\phi)
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

By equality of expectation every $|\chi\rangle$ has a place on the Bloch sphere.
\begin{equation*}
\langle\mathbf S\rangle=\langle\chi|\mathbf S|\chi\rangle=\langle s|\mathbf S|s\rangle
\end{equation*}
