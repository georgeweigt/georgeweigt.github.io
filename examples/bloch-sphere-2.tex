\section*{Bloch sphere 2}

Let $s$ be the general spin state
\begin{equation*}
s=\begin{pmatrix}
A_1e^{i\phi_1}
\\
A_2e^{i\phi_2}
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

Without loss of generality the $A$ coefficients can be made positive
with the overall signs determined by the phases.
\begin{equation*}
0\le A_1,\quad 0\le A_2
\end{equation*}

By unitarity of spin states
\begin{equation*}
s^\dagger s={A_1}^2+{A_2}^2=1
\end{equation*}

and
\begin{equation*}
|A_1|\le1,\quad|A_2|\le1
\end{equation*}

Hence there exists a $\theta$ such that
\begin{equation*}
s=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\,e^{i\phi_1}
\\[1ex]
\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\,e^{i\phi_2}
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

where
\begin{equation*}
0\le\theta\le\pi
\end{equation*}

Factor out the exponential $e^{i\phi_1}$.
\begin{equation*}
s=e^{i\phi_1}\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\frac{\theta}{2}
\\[1ex]
\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\,e^{i(\phi_2-\phi_1)}
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

By phase invariance $e^{i\phi_1}$ can be discarded.
\begin{equation*}
s=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\frac{\theta}{2}
\\[1ex]
\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\,e^{i(\phi_2-\phi_1)}
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

Substitute $\phi$ for $\phi_2-\phi_1$.
\begin{equation*}
s=\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\frac{\theta}{2}
\\[1ex]
\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\,e^{i\phi}
\end{pmatrix}
\end{equation*}

By the above derivation, every spin can be represented by a $\theta$ and a $\phi$ as in $s$.
Hence every spin has a Bloch sphere axis.
\begin{equation*}
\langle\mathbf S\rangle=\frac{\hbar}{2}\mathbf e_r+\theta\mathbf e_\theta+\phi\mathbf e_\phi
\end{equation*}
