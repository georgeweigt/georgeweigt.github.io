\section*{Moller scattering 4}

Here is another way to compute expected density.
Let
\begin{align*}
f_{11}&=32 (p_1\cdot p_2)^2 + 32 (p_1\cdot p_4)^2 - 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_2) + 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_4)
\\
f_{12}&=-32 (p_1\cdot p_2)^2 + 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_2)
\\
f_{22}&=32 (p_1\cdot p_2)^2 + 32 (p_1\cdot p_3)^2 - 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_2) + 64 m^2 (p_1\cdot p_3)
\end{align*}

Then
\begin{equation*}
\langle|\mathcal{M}|^2\rangle
=\frac{e^4}{4}
\left(\frac{f_{11}}{t^2}-\frac{2f_{12}}{tu}+\frac{f_{22}}{u^2}\right)
\end{equation*}

For $m=0$ we have
\begin{align*}
f_{11}&=32 (p_1\cdot p_2)^2 + 32 (p_1\cdot p_4)^2 & &=8s^2+8u^2
\\
f_{12}&=-32 (p_1\cdot p_2)^2 & &=-8s^2
\\
f_{22}&=32 (p_1\cdot p_2)^2 + 32 (p_1\cdot p_3)^2 & &=8s^2+8t^2
\end{align*}
