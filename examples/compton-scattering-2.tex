\section*{Compton scattering 2}

Scattering data from Table I of Compton's 1923 paper ($\Delta\lambda$ in Angstroms).
\begin{equation*}
\begin{matrix}
\text{Angle} & \Delta\lambda\,\text{obs.} & \Delta\lambda\,\text{calc.}
\\[1ex]
\phantom{0}45^\circ & 0.008 & 0.007
\\
\phantom{0}90^\circ & 0.021 & 0.025
\\
135^\circ & 0.046 & 0.041
\end{matrix}
\end{equation*}

Let us calculate $\Delta\lambda$ from the Compton equation
\begin{equation*}
\Delta\lambda=\lambda'-\lambda=\frac{h}{m_ec}\,(1-\cos\theta)
\end{equation*}

The results are
\begin{equation*}
\begin{matrix}
0.007
\\
0.024
\\
0.041
\end{matrix}
\end{equation*}
