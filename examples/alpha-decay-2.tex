\section*{Alpha decay 2}

Let $\nu$ be the rate of tunneling attempts.
Decay rate $\lambda$ is $\nu$ times tunneling probability.
\begin{equation*}
\lambda=\nu e^{-2\gamma}
\end{equation*}

Gamow theorized that $\nu$ is proportional to the inverse of $r_1$
because $\lambda$ increases as atomic weight decreases.
By dimensionality the numerator has to be a velocity $v$.
\begin{equation*}
\nu=\frac{v}{2r_1}
\end{equation*}

Velocity $v$ is unknown so let our hypothesis be that $v$ is proportional to $\sqrt{E/m}$.
\begin{equation*}
\lambda=\frac{\beta\sqrt{E/m}}{2r_1}e^{-2\gamma}
\end{equation*}

Coefficient $\beta$ is estimated to be
\begin{equation*}
\beta=843
\end{equation*}

Lifetime $\tau$ is the inverse of $\lambda$.
\begin{equation*}
\tau=\frac{1}{\lambda}
\end{equation*}

Half-life $\tau_{1/2}$ is a fraction of $\tau$.
\begin{equation*}
\tau_{1/2}=\frac{\log2}{\lambda}=\log2\frac{2r_1}{\beta\sqrt{E/m}}e^{2\gamma}
\end{equation*}

Hence
\begin{equation*}
\tau_{1/2}=\frac{2\log2}{\beta}\frac{r_1}{\sqrt{E/m}}
\exp\left(2C_1\frac{Z}{\sqrt E}-2C_2\sqrt{Zr_1}\right)
\end{equation*}

For the alpha decay process
${}^{238}_{\phantom{0}92}\mathrm U\rightarrow{}^{234}_{\phantom{0}90}\mathrm{Th}$
we have
\begin{align*}
A&=238
\\
Z&=90
\\
E&=4.27\,\text{MeV}
\\
r_1&=7.44\times10^{-15}\,\text{m}
\end{align*}

Hence
\begin{equation*}
\tau_{1/2}=3.9\times10^{17}\,\text{s}=12\times10^9\,\text{yr}
\end{equation*}

The actual half-life of ${}^{238}\mathrm U$ is
\begin{equation*}
4.5\times10^9\,\text{yr}
\end{equation*}
