\section*{Alpha decay 2}

Let $\nu$ be the rate of tunneling attempts.
Let $e^{-2\gamma}$ be the tunneling probability.
Then the decay rate $\lambda$ is
\begin{equation*}
\lambda=\nu e^{-2\gamma}
\end{equation*}

The half-life $\tau_{1/2}$ is
\begin{equation*}
\tau_{1/2}=\frac{\log2}{\lambda}=\frac{\log2}{\nu}\,e^{2\gamma}
\end{equation*}

Take the log of both sides.
\begin{equation*}
\log\tau_{1/2}=2\gamma-\log\nu+\log(\log2)
\end{equation*}

Gamow theorized that $\nu$ is proportional to $r_1$.
By the approximation $r_1\propto A^{1/3}$ we have
\begin{equation*}
\log\tau_{1/2}=2\left(C_1\frac{Z}{\sqrt{E}}-C_2\sqrt{Zr_1}\right)-C_3\log A+\log(\log2)
\end{equation*}

where $C_3$ is estimated to be
\begin{equation*}
C_3=10.1
\end{equation*}

For the alpha decay process
${}^{238}_{\phantom{0}92}\mathrm U\rightarrow{}^{234}_{\phantom{0}90}\mathrm{Th}$
we have
\begin{align*}
A&=238
\\
Z&=90
\\
E&=4.27\,\text{MeV}
\end{align*}

Hence
\begin{equation*}
\log\tau_{1/2}=39.9
\end{equation*}

and
\begin{equation*}
\tau_{1/2}=e^{39.9}\,\text{s}=2.2\times10^{17}\,\text{s}=7.0\times10^9\,\text{yr}
\end{equation*}

The actual half-life of ${}^{238}\mathrm U$ is
\begin{equation*}
4.5\times10^9\,\text{yr}
\end{equation*}
