\section*{Hydrogen energy four ways}

This is the traditional formula for $E_n$.
\begin{equation*}
E_n=-\frac{\mu}{2n^2}\left(\frac{e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0\hbar}\right)^2
\tag{1}
\end{equation*}

The following formula eliminates elementary charge $e$.
\begin{equation*}
E_n=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2n^2\mu a_0^2}
\tag{2}
\end{equation*}

The following $E_n$ can be divided by $\hbar$ to obtain angular frequency $\omega$.
\begin{equation*}
E_n=-\frac{\alpha\hbar c}{2n^2a_0}
\tag{3}
\end{equation*}

The following $E_n$ is irreducible.
\begin{equation*}
E_n=-\frac{\alpha^2\mu c^2}{2n^2}
\tag{4}
\end{equation*}

Equation (1) reduces to (4) by substituting
\begin{equation*}
e^2=4\pi\varepsilon_0\alpha\hbar c
\end{equation*}

Equations (2) and (3) reduce to (4) by substituting
\begin{equation*}
a_0=\frac{\hbar}{\alpha\mu c}
\end{equation*}
