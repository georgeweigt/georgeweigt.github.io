\section*{Relativistic energy}

Let $E$ be relativistic energy
\begin{equation*}
E=\sqrt{(mc^2)^2+(pc)^2}
\end{equation*}

where $p$ is relativistic momentum
\begin{equation*}
p=\frac{mv}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}
\end{equation*}

Expand $E$ as a Taylor series in $v$.
\begin{equation*}
E=mc^2+\frac{1}{2}mv^2-\frac{3mv^4}{8c^2}+\frac{5mv^6}{16c^4}-\frac{35mv^8}{128c^6}+\cdots
\end{equation*}

For $v\ll c$ use the approximation $v/c=0$.
\begin{equation*}
E=mc^2+\frac{1}{2}mv^2
\end{equation*}

Subtract rest-mass energy $mc^2$ to obtain classical kinetic energy.
\begin{equation*}
E=\frac{1}{2}mv^2
\end{equation*}
