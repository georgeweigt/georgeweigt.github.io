\section*{Relativistic energy}

Start with relativistic energy $E$.
\begin{equation*}
E=\sqrt{(mc^2)^2+(pc)^2}
\end{equation*}

Substitute $p=mv$.
\begin{equation*}
E=\sqrt{(mc^2)^2+(mvc)^2}
\end{equation*}

Expand $E$ as a Taylor series in $v$.
\begin{equation*}
E=mc^2+\frac{1}{2}mv^2-\frac{mv^4}{8c^2}+\frac{mv^6}{16c^4}-\frac{5mv^8}{128c^6}+\cdots
\end{equation*}

Let $v/c$ vanish for $v\ll c$.
\begin{equation*}
E=mc^2+\frac{1}{2}mv^2
\end{equation*}

Hence the classical kinetic energy
\begin{equation*}
E=\frac{1}{2}mv^2
\end{equation*}
